# Gravir.pro

**Personalized wooden gifts. Made in Bulgaria. Sold to Europe.**

---

## The Opportunity

The personalized gift market is worth **$30 billion** and growing 8% annually. In Bulgaria and Eastern Europe, there's no quality online option. We're changing that.

---

## What We Do

We engrave names, messages, and photos onto wooden products — cutting boards, plaques, coasters, phone stands — turning ordinary items into treasured memories.

**Customers**: Young parents, couples, pet owners, small businesses

---

## Why Now

1. **Bulgaria joined the Eurozone** (January 2026) — one currency, one market
2. **10% corporate tax** — lowest in EU
3. **Econt covers Romania & Greece** — regional shipping solved
4. **No major online competitor** — first mover advantage
5. **Laser equipment already owned** — near-zero CAPEX
