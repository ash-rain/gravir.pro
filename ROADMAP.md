## Key Principles

1. **Start small** — Prove it works
2. **Listen to customers** — Every feature requested
3. **Be fast** — Launch, improve, iterate
4. **Think long-term** — Build the brand
5. **Stay focused** — Do one thing well

---

## Risks

| Risk | Solution |

| Competition | Quality + speed |
