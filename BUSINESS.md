# Gravir.pro — Business Plan

## One Thing

We make gifts personal. That's it.

We're not "laser engraving." We're not "manufacturing." We're people who help others express feelings through wood.

---

## What We Do

We engrave names, messages, and photos onto wooden items. For moments that won't repeat.

### Who For
- Parents who want unique gifts for their children
- Couples who want a wedding keepsake
- Friends who value personal gestures  
- Businesses that want to impress

---

## Why Us

1. **Speed** — We do it in 24 hours. Others wait weeks.
2. **Quality** — You see the difference. Every letter is clear.
3. **Understanding** — We ask what the gift means to you.
4. **Easy** — You upload a photo or name — we do the magic.
